تجري قراءة نصوص صور الشيكات باستخدام أدوات Apple على الجهاز، دون إرسال الصور إلى خدمة قراءة خارجية. تعتمد دقة القراءة على الصورة واللغات والكتابة المدعومة، وتراجع الاقتراحات قبل حفظها.

القفل اختياري، ويستخدم مصادقة الجهاز المتاحة، مثل بصمة الوجه أو الإصبع أو رمز الجهاز. لا يتلقى التطبيق بيانات البصمة أو رمز الجهاز. القفل يحمي الوصول إلى واجهة التطبيق ويحجب معاينته، ولا يضيف كلمة مرور منفصلة لتشفير ملفات الشيكات.

تبقى البيانات حتى تحذف السجلات. تُزامَن عمليات الحذف عبر iCloud عند نجاح المزامنة. يمكنك إدارة مساحة iCloud وحساب Apple من إعدادات الجهاز. حذف التطبيق من جهاز واحد قد لا يزيل البيانات الموجودة في iCloud أو نسخ Apple الاحتياطية. تخضع خدمات iCloud والنسخ الاحتياطي لخياراتك وسياسات Apple.

## English

### Shekati privacy policy

Effective date: `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`  
Operator: `[LEGAL_OPERATOR_NAME]`  
Contact: `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`

Shekati organizes cheques on your iPhone. It stores the details you enter, such as amounts, cheque numbers, banks, parties, dates, notes, and images, locally on your device. When iCloud is available, records, currency and cheque images sync through the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. The app has no developer-operated server for your records and does not expose them to the developer.

Cheque image text is recognized with Apple's tools on the device, without sending images to an external recognition service. Recognition depends on the image and supported languages and handwriting. You review suggested details before saving them.

The app contains no advertising, usage analytics, or advertising tracking. Apple handles App Store purchases and services associated with your Apple Account and iCloud under its own policies. TestFlight feedback and Apple diagnostics may be subject to Apple's settings and your choices; the app does not forward them to a developer analytics service.

The camera is used when you request a cheque photograph. The photo picker lets you choose the images to attach. Notifications require your permission and are scheduled locally. They can show a cheque's type, amount and number; you can hide details in the app and manage lock-screen visibility in iPhone settings.

The optional lock uses available device authentication, including Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode. The app does not receive biometric data or your passcode. The lock protects access to the app interface and covers its app-switcher preview; it does not add a separate password that encrypts cheque files.

Records remain until you delete them. Deletions propagate through iCloud when syncing succeeds. You can manage iCloud storage and your Apple Account in device settings. Removing the app from one device may not remove records from iCloud or Apple's backups. iCloud and backup services are governed by your choices and Apple's policies.

If you contact support, you send the information you choose to share to `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`. Describe the issue and app/OS version, and redact financial information in screenshots. The information is used to address your request; the operator must set a support-message retention period in the final published policy.

For privacy questions, contact `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`. Policy changes will be reflected in the published version and its effective date.
